Step 1

Get it on your phone

Tap to open worshipplus.vercel.app ↗, then add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app and works offline.

iPhone — Safari
  • 1Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  • 2Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • 3Tap Add — the icon is on your phone.
Android — Chrome
  • 1Tap the ⋮ menu, top-right.
  • 2Tap Install app.
  • 3Confirm — the icon appears on your phone.

5Build a setlist
The songs for one service, in the order you'll play them.
  • 1In the Setlists tab, make a new setlist and give it a date.
  • 2Tap + Add Songs, search, and tick the ones you want.
  • 3Drag to reorder. Tap − to remove one.
Removing a song shows an Undo — nothing disappears by accident.

6Perform
Big, clean, hands-free — made for leading live.
  • 1Open a song and go full-screen.
  • 2Swipe, or tap Prev / Next, through the pages.
  • 3Tap the metronome for tempo, or Markup to scribble notes.
Prev / Next light up when there's a page that way, and grey out at the start or end.

7Teams — share with your group
Bring people in and keep everyone on the same charts.
  • 1Make a team, then tap Add Member to invite people.
  • 2Put songs in Shared Songs for the whole team to use.
  • 3Share a setlist — everyone sees the same order.
Only leaders can delete a shared setlist — so nobody removes it by mistake.
